FAQ

Questions I get most often

What's the minimum monthly ad budget you work with?

I work with monthly budgets starting at $500 (or equivalent). Below that it's tough to hit a meaningful result because Meta needs enough data to learn.

Do you guarantee a specific ROAS or result?

No. Anyone guaranteeing numbers before seeing your account, product and market is lying. What I do guarantee: a clear plan, disciplined work, and full transparency in the numbers every week.

Do you work monthly or per project?

Mostly monthly retainers, because optimization takes time. But I also take fixed-scope projects or Audit + Strategy engagements. We'll agree based on your case.

If I don't have a store or website yet, can you help?

Yes — I have a team that builds e-commerce stores (Shopify / Salla) and landing pages. But that's not my core service. My core is ads and analytics.

How fast do you reply?

Form or WhatsApp — usually within 24 hours, sometimes same-day depending on my schedule.
